🎓 Project Conclusions & Rural Development Recommendations
1. Local Income Generation

Skilled villagers (tailors, electricians, tutors, craftsmen) can earn ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 in supplementary monthly income without leaving their home community.

2. Women & Youth Upliftment

Provides productive vacation utilization for students (learning computers & spoken English) and equips rural women with tailoring and micro-enterprise abilities.

3. Circular Rural Economy

Money circulates within the village instead of flowing out to commercial urban institutions, strengthening community cohesion and rural self-reliance.

🏡 GramKushal

A grassroots community platform connecting skilled village mentors with local learners to promote self-employment, women empowerment, and rural development.

Project Objectives

  • • Mapping and utilizing existing village talents
  • • Affordable and accessible offline hands-on skill training
  • • Fostering local self-employment for youth and women
  • • Retaining economic wealth and opportunities within the community

Accessibility & Inclusive Design

🔊 Voice Assistance: Read aloud cards and instructions for low-literacy users.

🖼️ Picture Mode: High contrast colors and large intuitive symbols for easy navigation.

📞 Direct Contact: Instant one-tap phone calls and WhatsApp to local teachers.
